## Summary

Full-parameter supervised fine-tuning of Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct on 15,000 advanced-selected AceReason examples. Seven-benchmark average pass@8 is 51.52%, compared with 49.55% for the base model. The report describes the training configuration, data selection, benchmark results and limitations.

The repositories are private and require grader access.
